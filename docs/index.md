# PiNetBeacon

Welcome! 👋

PiNetBeacon is a small, Raspberry Pi–friendly project that helps you **watch your home internet** and learn a bit more about how networks behave along the way.

If you've ever wondered:

- “Is my internet actually flaky, or is it just that one site?”
- “What does *latency* really mean for me?”
- “How often is my connection dropping when I'm not looking?”

...you’re in the right place.
